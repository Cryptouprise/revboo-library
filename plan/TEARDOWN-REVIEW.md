# Teardown ad review (Oct 4, 2026)

Before/after "your ad vs what it should be" ads. Method: 16-frame contact sheets + first-2-s sheets, faster-whisper small.en, ffmpeg loudnorm (integrated LUFS), silencedetect. Working files: `/workspace/revboo/review-oct4/` on the box. Judged against [BRAND.md](../BRAND.md).

**Big picture:** the concepts are good; the *finish* is out of date. Every pre-Oct-4 teardown uses the now-banned all-caps Anton captions and paraphrases the tagline ("media guy / give him"). None has a 4:5 cut. Most run 30-40 s, about 10-15 s longer than the story needs. Audio is fine (all ≈ -14 LUFS except Ad Autopsy). They are music + text with no VO, so there are no VO word errors ("abs"/"Revo") to fix, and they read fine sound-off. Fix = house re-caption + trim + 4:5, not a rebuild.

## Notes on specific issues
- **Tagline drift:** Scroll Past, Tube Man, Hammer Broke, Graveyard say "media guy / him / bury". The approved line is "Don't fire your media buyer. Give them better ads." Use it verbatim on re-caption (Graveyard's "Don't bury…" pun can stay as a variant if Chase likes it).
- **Fire-logo ending:** fine on Revboo's own ads (BRAND.md bans it on *client* ads only).
- **No end card in the older ads carries the offer** except Ad Doctor. Add "First 2 ads free. Delivered in 24 hours." to every Revboo end card.
- **Clone War Room** (not a teardown): native Seedance VO, -15.6 LUFS (raise ~1.5 dB). The line "You started a business, not a production company" is strong for owners.
- **Kinetic Type Reel take 2:** -17.2 LUFS, too quiet. **New Hat hook_A** (the original upload): -16.3 LUFS with a burned-in caps hook. Use the captioned version.
