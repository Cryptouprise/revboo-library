# UPCOMING: what to build next

## Hooks that get the click (first 2 s, built from what works in our library)
Patterns: an absurd visual from frame 1 + a short line that names the viewer's pain. No numbers we can't prove.
1. **Burning money:** "Your ad budget, every week." over the furnace/cash shot (proven: Furnace).
2. **Call-out to the buyer:** "Media buyers: it's not your targeting." / "Don't fire your media buyer."
3. **Relationship/comedy:** "Your ad wants more budget. Again." (New Hat therapy).
4. **Before/after split:** "Your ad. / What it should be." Tube Man, Remake (same product, same budget, different ad).
5. **Diagnosis:** "Your ads aren't dying because of the offer." (Muse #01 line, Ad Doctor).
6. **Scroll proof:** the thumb flicking past a stiff ad ("They scrolled. You paid for that.").
7. **Offer-first:** "We'll make your next 2 ads free. In 24 hours." For warm/retargeting audiences.
8. **Niche call-outs:** "PI attorneys: your ad looks like everyone else's." / "HVAC owners: …". One line, one niche per ad set.
