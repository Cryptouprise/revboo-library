## 1. What the research says

### Meta's own ranking signals (primary sources)
- **Feed predicts conversation, not just likes.** Meta predicts "how likely the post is to spark a conversation or back and forth discussion," how likely you are to share it, time spent viewing comments, and "the predicted amount of additional comments or replies a post will get if you comment or share." Feed signals include the **average length of comments** and **how relevant the comments are to the topic**. → [Our approach to Facebook Feed ranking](https://transparency.meta.com/features/ranking-and-content), [Facebook Feed](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-feed)
- **Comments that ask a question or invite a reply rank higher**, and so do posts whose author replies to comments. → [Facebook Feed ranked comments](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-feed-ranked-comments/). **We reply to every comment, ideally in the first hour.**
- **Reels are ranked on watching**: will you watch rather than skip, will you watch to completion, and how many others finished it. → [Facebook Reels](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-reels). Facebook Video also uses "videos you spent less than three seconds watching" as a signal. → [Facebook Video](https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-video/)
- **Engagement bait gets demoted**: "Posts that explicitly request engagement (such as votes, shares, comments, tags, likes, or other reactions) for purposes other than a specific call to action." Pages that do it repeatedly get demoted harder. → [Meta: Engagement bait](https://transparency.meta.com/en-gb/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait), summary at [FYProof](https://fyproof.com/guides/engagement-bait-penalties). "Comment YES" and "tag someone who…" are named patterns. A real question ("What would you do here?") or a genuine specific CTA ("post your ad and we'll remake one") is fine.

### Hooks (research)
- Curiosity works best in the middle: a meta-analysis of 8,977 headline A/B tests found clicks peak at moderate concreteness, and both too vague and too specific lose. → [Scientific Reports, "When curiosity gaps backfire"](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-81575-9). **Tease the payoff and name the subject. Never stay fully vague.**
- In a small study of 80 TikTok videos, curiosity and pain-point hooks beat direct informational openers. → [Inspira Journals](https://inspirajournals.com/uploads/Issues/2123118371.pdf). This is directional only.

## 2. The hook formula

Line 1 is the hook. Facebook cuts feed captions after about 2-3 lines ("See more"), so line 1 has to work alone. **Use 10 words or fewer. No brand name. No disclaimer.** Pick one pattern:

1. **Contrarian flip**: "Your ad isn't dead. It's stale." / "Your offer is fine. Your ad is boring."
2. **Scene drop**: start mid-story. "You called a meeting about the ad. Everyone in the room was you."
3. **Direct challenge**: "You get 12 seconds. Go."
4. **Specific curiosity**: name the subject, hold back the payoff. "Where good ad budgets go to die."
5. **Hot take label**: "Hot take: …" (use at most once a week, or it stops working)

The on-image hook (graphic headline or video frame 1) must make sense with the sound off and must not repeat line 1 word for word. The two should work as a pair.

## 3. Caption structure (60-90 words)
1. **Hook** (line 1)
2. **Story or joke** (2-3 short lines): tell what happens in the video, or land the joke. Don't pitch.
3. **The human line, once**, in the middle or near the end, never as the opener. For example: "Our human editors built the whole cemetery. AI did the fog." Make it part of the joke when you can.
4. **One question** that is easy to answer and has options or a number (see section 4)
5. Optional **soft offer** on about 1 in 3 posts: "First 2 are free."
6. Sign-off: **"Real editors. AI speed. Fresh ads every week."**

## 4. Comment drivers
- **Questions with low-effort answers**: pick one (clone A/B/C, left or right), a number (0-3 boxes checked, weeks running), or a fill-in ("what would its headstone say?"). Avoid yes/no.
- **Hot takes that challenge the industry** (once a week): "Most 'offer problems' are creative problems," "More budget doesn't fix a tired ad," "Your media buyer isn't the problem." End with "Agree or disagree? Tell us why." Reply to every disagreement with substance, never sarcasm.
- **Remake Your Worst Ad (weekly series, every Friday)**:
  1. Episode post: "Post a screenshot or link of the ad you're least proud of, plus one line on what you sell."
  2. The editors pick 1 submission a week (with the owner's OK in DMs, since it's their ad).
  3. The following week, post a before/after Reel or carousel: the original, the remake, and the 3 things we changed. Credit and tag the business only if they agree.
  4. The picked business gets the remake free (this counts toward "First 2 ads free").
  5. Number the episodes (EP. 01, 02…) on the graphic so it reads as a series.
  - **Needs Chase's sign-off before launch** (capacity for 1 public remake a week, and permission rules).
- **"Comment STALE and we'll roast yours"**: allowed only when we actually do it. Every "STALE" gets a reply within 24 h with a 1-2 line roast and one fix. Meta's rule allows a *specific* call to action, but a keyword-only ask sits close to "comment baiting," so (a) use it at most once every 2 weeks, (b) say what they get ("we'll reply with one thing to fix"), and (c) prefer "post your ad" over "comment WORD."
- **Never use**: "tag someone who," "like if," "share if," "comment YES," or anything gated ("follow to get").

## 5. Visual rules for graphics (1080x1350, 4:5)
- **Never plain text on black.** Each graphic uses one of: a full-bleed real frame from an approved final (mascot, before/after, fire), a heavy orange #FF4B0A glow or diagonal texture, or a photo behind type.
- **One giant idea**: headline at 120-190 px in Inter Tight 800, warm white #FAF8F5, **one** orange accent word. It should be readable at thumbnail size from across a room.
- **One "pattern interrupt" element**: a rotated STALE stamp, a diagonal before/after split, an oversized quote mark, or a "NEW SERIES · EP. 01" chip.
- Keep 64 px margins. No text in the top 120 px where the FB header crowds it, and no baked-in video headline behind our type (crop or gradient it out).
- Wordmark bottom-left (Revboo + orange .video), "Real editors. AI speed." bottom-right.
- **No people we made up, no fake names, no Chase on camera** (for now). The mascot is the cardboard cutout guy.
- Videos: use frame 1 or the approved cover. The thumbnail must make sense muted.

## 6. 10/10 checklist (1 point each)
1. **Hook**: line 1 is 10 words or fewer, works alone, and isn't a disclaimer
2. **Thumb-stop visual**: the frame or graphic has a pattern interrupt and passes the thumbnail test
3. **Story or joke, not pitch**: the body tells what happens or lands a joke
4. **Human team mentioned exactly once**, not in line 1
5. **A specific question** that is easy to answer (options, number or fill-in)
6. **No engagement bait** under Meta's rules
7. **Brand-safe**: no stats, prices or client claims; one orange word; revboo.video spelled right
8. **Offer discipline**: "free" only on the planned 1-in-3 posts, said softly
9. **Shareable or saveable**: a reason to send it to a business-owner friend (a joke, a test, or a take)
10. **Clean render**: no clipping, no baked-in text collisions, caption at 90 words or fewer, sign-off present

A score below 8 means rewrite before Chase reviews it.

## 7. Metrics to track (Meta Business Suite → Insights → Content)
| Metric | Why | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| **3-second view rate** (3 s views ÷ impressions) | Hook strength. Meta ranks on skip vs watch. | Compare posts against each other. The first 2 weeks set our baseline. |
| **Average watch time and completion %** | The core Reels signal (watch to completion) | Where the retention curve drops |
| **Comments** (count and average length) | Meta weighs conversation and comment length | Real answers vs emoji-only replies |
| **Shares** (including sends to Messenger) | Strongest reach signal for recommendations | Which formats get sent |
| **Link clicks** (revboo.video) | Intent | Clicks per 1,000 impressions |
| **Free-ad requests** (DMs, form, Remake submissions) | The business outcome | Requests per post and per series episode |
| Negative feedback (hides, "see less") | Demotion risk | Any spike after a hot take or keyword ask |
