# FLAGSHIP-SCRIPTS-V2.md: Planet Ad, Monday Night, The Set (shot-by-shot rewrite)

> Oct 16, 2026. Replaces the scripts in [FLAGSHIP-CONCEPTS.md](FLAGSHIP-CONCEPTS.md) after Chase's notes on the Oct 15 drafts (`edits/flagship-drafts/`). Scripts only. Nothing was generated and no money was spent.

## What changed (Chase's notes → rules)

1. **"One Line, Every Genre" is dropped.** No repeated-line gimmicks anywhere.
2. **One narrator per spot.** No two-voice back-and-forth, no VO over VO, no dialogue on top of narration. The end clip's audio starts only after the last VO line ends.
3. **One caption on screen at a time.** Each caption is the exact VO line (or a short cut of it), and it never stacks on burned-in text. The Monday m4 teardown stack and the Set t5 white-on-white are fixed by design: teardown shots carry no house caption.
4. **No pretty-for-nothing shots.** Every shot dramatizes one business pain (scrolled past, money burning on stale creative, competitor's fresh ad winning, media buyer starved of creative) or Revboo fixing it. No river crossings, sunrises or flyovers.
5. **No line is said twice.** The sign-off and tagline appear once each per spot.
6. **Core message in every spot:** making fresh video creative is hard, slow, and the tools keep changing, so businesses quit. Revboo is a real human creative team that uses AI for speed (**not a SaaS tool, never shown as an app or dashboard you log into**) and finds winners faster through constant testing.
7. **Locked copy:** sign-off "Real editors. AI speed. Fresh ads every week." · tagline "Keep your media buyer. Your ads deserve better creative." · offer "First 2 ads free." · price "Starting at $2k/month." (end card only, once).
8. Brand rules still apply: Style C captions, one orange word (*marked*) per caption, no stats on screen, Cutout/Owner/Media Buyer cast, fire-logo end clip with the watermark cropped.
