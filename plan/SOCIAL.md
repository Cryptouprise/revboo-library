## Reuse notes

- **Instagram:** post the 9:16 as a Reel on the same day/time (or cross-post from Meta Business Suite). Put the CTA as "Link in bio → revboo.video" (IG captions don't link). Use the 4:5 for grid posts. Up to 3-5 niche hashtags at the end: #videoads #mediabuyer #facebookads #ugcads #adcreative.
- **TikTok:** 9:16 only, post 7-9 PM MT. Trim the caption to the hook line + "revboo.video in bio". Best fits: Tube Man, Ad Autopsy, Graveyard, Clone War Room, Best-of. Pick TikTok sounds only if they don't cover the VO (the ads must read muted anyway).
- **LinkedIn:** 4:5 natively uploaded, Tue-Thu 7:30 AM MT. Lead with the agency/media-buyer angle (Furnace, New Hat, Ad Doctor, Remake). Use a longer caption: 2-3 short lines on why creative fatigue happens, then the offer and revboo.video. No hashtag stacks.
- **Rules everywhere:** don't add your own on-screen text over the videos (BRAND.md safe zones), no stats/prices, and spell **revboo.video** letter by letter.
